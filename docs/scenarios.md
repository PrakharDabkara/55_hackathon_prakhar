# Scenarios

The interactions between features that matter most, with the behaviour we expect. Each has a test; `scenarios.int.test.ts` is `apps/api/src/scenarios.int.test.ts`. Change the behaviour on purpose, then the scenario and its test with it.

A shop that closes ("Dukaan aaj band hai") stops getting **new** pings and orders and is left out of learned stock. What is already in flight stays: its open cards and orders can still be answered.

## Found and fixed while writing these

- S9: a shop that had closed was still pushed "Naya sawaal" for a ping that arrived after (fixed in `notifier.ts`).
- S13: the plan dropped the shop's "thoda/bahut hai"; plan items now carry `quantityHint` and the card shows it.
- S1 itself was missing: a shop that opened late was never told about requests already running.
- S2/S3 are decisions rather than fixes: closing keeps commitments in flight (see the note above the table).
