FOR BUYERS

Compare every shop nearby. Pick the best.

Nearby shops reply with their price. You compare the prices from every nearby shop and pick the best, with no walking to ten shops.

  1. Write your parchi. One thing, or a whole list. Say it the way you'd say it at the counter: charging cable, or phone wire.
  2. Shops nearby answer. Each shop sees only what it sells, and replies with its price and whether it delivers.
  3. You choose. We plan the fewest shops and the shortest walk. Pick up from one, have another deliver. Pay cash.

Your details stay private. Shops see what you asked, never your number, and a first name only if you type one. Your address goes to a shop only once it accepts your order.

An AI agent that plans your errand

Tell it a job, like "party supplies for Sunday". It builds the list, asks the shops nearby, and plans the fewest shops and the shortest route. You choose; nothing is ordered until you choose.

  • Tell it the job

    Write it in English, Hindi or Hinglish. No need to list every item.

  • It builds the list

    It suggests the usual items for the job and you tick what you need.

  • It asks the shops

    It checks which shops nearby already have each item, and asks the rest. If it is not sure what you mean, it asks you.

  • Fewest shops, shortest route

    You get two plans: the cheapest, and the fewest shops. Stops come in route order.

  • Never guesses prices

    Prices and stock come only from the shops' answers.

Why shops join

  • No catalogue. No inventory sheet.

    Your stock list builds itself as you answer requests and sell.

  • Your price, your rules

    You set the price on every answer. Offer delivery when you can, with your own fee.

  • Know what your area wants

    See what people nearby asked for today, and what you missed.

  • Lists, packed right

    A shopping list arrives as one parchi with the items that fit your shop. Tick them off as you pack.

Get your shop online in a few easy steps

  1. Log in with your phone number

    Enter your number and a one-time code. No password, no long form.

  2. Put your shop on the map

    Add your shop's name and logo, pin where it is, and pick what you sell.

  3. Answer from the counter

    When someone nearby asks for something you sell, it arrives on your phone. In stock, your price, send.
