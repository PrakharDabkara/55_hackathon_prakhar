Open your shop in three steps

  1. Log in with your phone number

    Enter your number and a one-time code. No password, no long form.

  2. Put your shop on the map

    Add your shop's name and logo, pin where it is, and pick what you sell.

  3. Answer from the counter

    When someone nearby asks for something you sell, it arrives on your phone. In stock, your price, send.

Why shops join

  • No catalogue to build

    Your shelf builds itself from every answer you give.

  • Your price, your rules

    You set the price on every answer. Offer delivery when you can, with your own fee.

  • Know what your area wants

    See what people nearby asked for today, and what you missed.

  • Customers stay private

    You see what was asked, never the customer's number, and a first name only if they type one. Their address comes only once the order is accepted.

  • Lists, packed right

    A shopping list arrives as one parchi with the items that fit your shop. Tick them off as you pack.

FOR BUYERS

Ask the shops nearby, then buy.

No waiting days for a parcel. No padding a cart to beat a fee. Ask the shops you already walk past.

  1. Write your parchi. One thing, or a whole list. The way you'd say it at the counter: maida or refined flour, mirchi or chillies.
  2. Shops nearby answer. Each shop sees only what it sells, and replies with its price and whether it delivers.
  3. You choose. We plan the fewest shops and the shortest walk. Pick up from one, have another deliver. Pay cash.

AI that asks before it acts

AI understands everyday names like maida and mirchi, sorts a shopping list in one go, and plans errands. It suggests; people decide.

  • Shops get clear requests

    "Mirchi" could be fresh chillies or powder, so it checks with the buyer before it reaches you.

  • Only shops that sell it

    A request goes to shops that carry that kind of item, not to everyone.

  • Buyers confirm the list

    Nothing goes to a shop until the buyer has checked it.

  • Never guesses prices

    Prices and stock come only from your answers.
